Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before you start writing with Writez.

What can I write with Writez?

Writez gives you a flexible space for ideas, drafts and longer pieces that benefit from a clear chapter-based structure.

Does Writez support Markdown?

Yes. Choose a Markdown toolbar or a familiar text-formatting toolbar. You can also rearrange shortcuts around the way you write.

Which file formats can I export?

Writez supports export to PDF, Markdown, HTML and Docs formats.

Can I restore an earlier draft?

Yes. Version History lets you review earlier changes and restore the version you need.

Does Version History sync with Google Drive?

No. Version History is stored locally on the current device and is separate from Google Drive sync and backup.
